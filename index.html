Stadium Guidelines

Ticket Verification

E-tickets must be downloaded or printed clearly. Barcodes will be scanned at the gate entrance turnstiles.

ID Proof Requirement

Carry a valid government-issued photo ID identity proof matching the name on your booking records.

Prohibited Items

Bags, power banks, water bottles, helmets, banners, and professional cameras are completely restricted inside the stadium.
